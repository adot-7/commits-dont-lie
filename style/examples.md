#Post 4
I built Kahani.
It is an interactive world of fables built using world models by @reactorworld
 and narrations using @FishAudio

Through these stories, the child can take whatever actions he/she wants and learn the morals as consequences of those actions.

It's like stepping into those old kahaniya/stories your parents used to tell you. But here, you can choose your actions and see reap the fruits of those actions

Built for the #inception2 hackathon organized by @thelaunchd
 @raina_sai
 @MaxMill06
 and team.
Loved being a part of this!
